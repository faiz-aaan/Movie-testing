# Treatment: "Quiet Desk" test (City Symphony, 20 s, 1080x1920, 24 fps)

Subject: one small office room, filmed on a phone (your footage). One subject: the desk, one goal: the room waits for work to begin.

Candidate structures
1. Chronological: wide room -> desk -> headphones -> window -> empty room. Clear, but flat.
2. Rhythm first: cut the room into 8 beats of rising density, then one slow hold. Clean music-driven shape.
3. Stillness: long slow shots only, one fast cut at the end. Quiet but the 20 s is short for it.

Choice: 2. Cuts sit on the beat, speed bends time (fast on the room, slow on the headphones and the window), and the film ends on a hard cut to black on a downbeat. No narrator; the score carries it.

Shot plan (source seconds -> edit)
- 0.0-2.6 s wide of room: time-lapse x4 (cloud/light motion through the window)
- 3.0-5.8 s desk and laptop: normal speed, cut on beat
- 6.0-9.0 s headphones: slow motion x0.5 (the one held detail)
- 9.5-12.8 s TV and chairs: x2, cut on beat
- 13.0-17.0 s window with trees: slow x0.5, the held image
- 17.4-19.5 s chairs and room: x3, quick cuts on the 8th-notes
- 19.5-20.0 s hard cut to black on the downbeat

Not in this film: the upside-down phone shots (last 8 s of the footage). Sound: original, synthesised; no music from others.
Target loudness: -14 LUFS (mux.sh).

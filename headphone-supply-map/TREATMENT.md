# TREATMENT: "Where a Headphone Comes From" (3D Map Explainer, 34 s, English voice)

**Topic.** The supply chain behind a typical wireless headphone, told as a map. Typical, not any one brand: routes are schematic and say so on screen.

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **Funnel** (world -> one hub -> back out). The demo's shape. Clear, but it is the style's cliché.
2. **Journey along one line**: follow one part from mine to buyer. Poor fit: a headphone is many parts from many places, not one.
3. **A list that turns**: three dependencies light up one by one on the globe (magnets, battery metal, chips), each as a pillar with its share; then all of it **converges** on one assembly region, and the lines fan back out to buyers. The list teaches, the turn is the convergence.

**Chosen: 3.** The story *is* convergence: three far-apart places that most listeners have never heard of, all needed for one object. A funnel would hide that.

### 2. Logline and arc
*A pair of headphones needs three things from three places, and one region pulls them together.*
- **Setup (0-4.4 s):** a low shot over the Pearl River Delta, one amber point pinned on the hub and a title set in the sky; the camera climbs and the region opens up.
- **List (4.4-21 s):** magnets, battery metal, chips: a pillar grows in each place, slab by slab, with its share beside it.
- **Turn (22-26 s):** a breath of near silence, then three lines ignite and converge on the Pearl River Delta; the camera dives to it.
- **Ending (26-34 s):** two schematic lanes leave the hub (to North America, to Europe); the camera pulls out to the wide map and holds the three pillars and the lit hub, then fades.

## 8. Rules for this film
- **No borders drawn.** Imagery and terrain only. Taiwan appears only as a place; never listed as a country. Narration says "Taiwan-based foundry", never "country".
- **Facts** only as listed in `FACTS.md`, each with source and basis. Routes are labelled **schematic**.
- **Data**: AWS Terrain Tiles (free with attribution) and **Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (CC BY 4.0)**. The newer EOX releases are non-commercial: not used.

## 9. How this film differs from the demo (needs 4 of 6; this has all 6)
Structure: list that turns vs funnel. Opening: low ground shot vs globe from orbit. Signature shot: three lines converging on a lit hub vs the cut-open sea. Camera: crane up then slow orbit then dive then pull-out, no globe at all. Ending: wide map holding the pillars vs globe turning east. Score: own key and tempo (A minor, 90 bpm vs D minor, 96) and own palette (harp, hang, hand chimes, horn).

## 10. Honest notes
- Heights are exaggerated x3.2 (stated on screen). Pillar heights are not to scale; pillar and lane positions are schematic and say so.
- The IEA figures could not be opened directly (HTTP 403); they are verified only through press coverage that quotes them (see FACTS.md).
